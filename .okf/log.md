# Change Log

## 2026-10-17

* Created `siftasr` shared library with confidence-gated language detection, per-language ASR routing (`en`, `vi`, `zh`, Whisper fallback), `pyobjc` Core ML runner, SSH remote pipe, and word/turn speaker attribution (`architecture/routing-and-speakers.md`, `getting-started.md`).
* **Updated**: [Routing and speaker attribution](architecture/routing-and-speakers.md) - merged Parakeet BPE subwords into whole words with short-orphan cue folding (`parakeet_tokens_to_words`, `fold_short_cues`) and upgraded `cluster_embeddings` to two-pass anchor seeding (`>= 2.5 s`), splinter folding (`< 8 s`), sandwich smoothing, and sub-`0.8 s` backchannel continuity fill after benchmarking on a 55-minute lecture + 5-questioner Q&A.
* **Updated**: [Routing and speaker attribution](architecture/routing-and-speakers.md) - cut 55-minute end-to-end peak RSS by 46% (`1,144 MB -> 622 MB`), CPU time by 21%, and wall time by 23 s via single-allocation `decode_audio`, cached encoder-only LID, direct in-memory `150 s / 4 s` `_parakeet_mlx` chunking with per-chunk `mx.clear_cache()`, $O(N)$ `split_quiet`, and two-stage sparse boundary embedding in `voices()`.
* **Updated**: [Routing and speaker attribution](architecture/routing-and-speakers.md) - added compiled speculative TDT greedy decoding (`_decode_tdt_fast` in `src/siftasr/asr.py`) and bounded ONNX diarization worker concurrency (`src/siftasr/speakers.py`), cutting 55m 36s end-to-end wall time to `98.9 s` (`33.7× RT`), CPU time to `84.5 s` (`-45.8%` vs `v0.1`), and keeping diarization within the ASR peak RSS ceiling (`629.5 MB`).
* **Added**: [Performance and speaker diarization](decisions/performance-and-diarization.md) - documented root causes and benchmarks for compiled speculative TDT decoding, 150 s / 4 s in-memory chunking, single-allocation PCM decoding, and two-stage sparse anchor-seeded speaker clustering.
